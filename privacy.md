# Privacy Policy

This app only stores the TikTok access token and refresh token needed to post videos on your behalf.

We do not:
- Sell or share your data with third parties.
- Access your TikTok account beyond the video.publish and video.upload scopes.
- Store your videos permanently (they are only uploaded to TikTok).

Tokens are stored as encrypted GitHub Actions secrets. You can revoke access at any time.
